Process

How a project runs

A clear four-step process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We start with a conversation about your business, your customers, and the result you need. We map the problem, agree on scope, and give you a clear plan and estimate before any code is written.

  2. 2

    Build

    We design and build your product in working stages, sharing progress as we go. You see real, usable features early — not a big reveal at the end — and feedback shapes the next stage.

  3. 3

    Launch

    We test, deploy, and launch your product properly: live on your domain, working on phones and desktops, with the essentials set up so customers can use it from day one.

  4. 4

    Grow

    After launch we look at what the numbers say — sales, sign-ups, time saved — and improve what matters. New features and automations are added where they will have the most impact.
