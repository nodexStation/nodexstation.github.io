How it works

One agent, wired into
the tools you already use.

Your customers talk to you where they already talk to you. The agent listens there, thinks with your business rules, and acts in your systems — no new app for anyone to learn.

  1. Step 01 — Connect

    Your channels plug in

    Messenger, your store, your inbox and your forms become one stream of work the agent can see.

  2. Step 02 — Understand · Decide · Act

    The agent does the task

    It understands what was asked, decides using your prices, policies and stock, then acts — not just a reply, the job finished.

  3. Step 03 — Outcomes

    You see results, not chores

    Replies sent, orders updated, leads in your hands by morning — with a record of everything the agent did.

Input Customer messages, orders, enquiries — from Messenger, your store, email and web forms.
Process The agent understands intent, checks live business data, decides with your rules, and acts end-to-end.
Output Instant replies, automatic order updates, captured leads, and a log you can review any time.

Use cases

Clothing brand
support automation

Problem

The owner answers the same ten questions himself, on Messenger, at midnight — about ten hours every week.

Automation

An FAQ agent trained on sizes, prices, delivery and cash-on-delivery answers instantly, and captures the lead when a customer is ready to buy.

Outcome

Roughly ten hours a week back — forty hours a month — and no enquiry left unanswered overnight.

E-commerce
order updates

Problem

“Where is my order?” is the loudest question in the inbox, and every unanswered one risks a cancelled order.

Automation

The agent watches order status and messages the customer at each step — confirmed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered.

Outcome

Customers stop asking because they already know. Support volume drops; orders complete.

service businesses

Problem

Automation

An agent greets them, answers the basics, and takes a name and number instead of losing the conversation.

Outcome

Every night-time enquiry becomes a lead on your list by morning — not a sale handed to a competitor.
