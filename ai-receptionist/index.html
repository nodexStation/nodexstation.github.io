Questions

Before you ask.

Does it really work at 2am?

Yes. The chat receptionist is on your website around the clock. It answers questions, takes booking requests and takes messages at any hour — your customers get a reply in seconds, and you find everything in your email in the morning.

What do you need from me?

Your services and prices, your opening hours, and your contact details — plus access to add the receptionist to your website. That's it. We do the building and the setup.

Will it answer with the wrong prices?

No — it only answers from the information you give us. If a customer asks something it doesn't know, it takes a message for your team instead of guessing.

Can it answer my phone calls?

The chat version on this page works on your website. A live phone-voice version is a separate setup — ask us and we'll talk through what it needs and what it costs.

Is there really no monthly fee?

Right — the chat receptionist is a one-time setup. No subscription, no per-message charges. If you later want changes to your services or prices, small updates are quick and we'll quote them plainly.

What kind of business is it for?

Any local business that gets the same questions and booking requests every day — dental and medical clinics, salons, garages, trades, restaurants, estate agents. If your customers ask and book, it fits.
